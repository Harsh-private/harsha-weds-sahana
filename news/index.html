Local Couple To Tie The Knot

Families overjoyed; evening reception and dawn muhurtham announced; entire town invited.

The mandap, photographed the night before.

ANANTAPUR — In news that surprised absolutely no one, Harsha and Sahana have announced that they will marry this November. Celebrations begin with a reception and dinner on Wednesday, 25 November, at 7 PM. The muhurtham follows at dawn on Thursday, 26 November, between 4:30 and 5:30 AM, in the Subha Thula Lagnam. Both events will be held at S J R Function Hall on Bangalore Road. “We want everyone there,” the couple said in a joint statement. Sources close to the families confirm that gifts are not required. Blessings, however, are.
